PBL4 Group 1 – Project Wiki

Welcome to the PBL4 Group 1 project wiki.

This wiki serves as the central location for project information, team responsibilities, meeting records, important decisions, progress updates, and relevant project resources.

1. Project Overview

PBL4 Group 1 is working on a software development project involving image alignment and blending.

The project involves designing and implementing the software, conducting experiments and testing, preparing technical documentation, and coordinating work across different departments.

The team is divided into three departments:

  • Development: Software implementation, UML design, integration, experiments, and testing.
  • Documentation: Project explanations, code comments, setup instructions, results, and Doxygen documentation.
  • Management: Project planning, task coordination, workload tracking, meeting records, and final delivery.

The departments work together to complete the project and prepare the final presentation.
